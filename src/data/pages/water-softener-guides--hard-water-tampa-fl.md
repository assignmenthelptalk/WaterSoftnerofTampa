## What Do Tampa Homeowners Need to Know About Hard Water?

Three questions cover the basics: what hard water is, why Tampa water is hard and how hard it measures.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">

### What Is Hard Water?

Hard water is water with a high concentration of dissolved calcium and magnesium. These minerals dissolve as water moves through rock and soil, and they stay in the water until something removes them. Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate or in grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 as moderately hard, 121 to 180 as hard and over 180 as very hard.

### Why Is Tampa Water Hard?

Tampa water is hard because the river it comes from carries minerals from limestone groundwater. The Tampa Water Department's 2025 water quality report says customers rely on the Hillsborough River as their primary source, supplemented by 980 million gallons of treated water stored in aquifers and recovery wells and, when necessary, water bought from Tampa Bay Water, which was 2.7 percent of Tampa's drinking water in 2025. According to a 2010 U.S. Geological Survey report titled “A Geochemical Mass-Balance Method for Base-Flow Separation, Upper Hillsborough River Watershed, West-Central Florida, 2003-2005 and 2009,” the surface water in the upper Hillsborough River basin is a calcium-bicarbonate type water that closely resembles groundwater, which indicates that much of it comes from local groundwater discharge. The same report found that the discharge is diluted by precipitation and runoff in the wet season but retains the calcium-bicarbonate characteristics of Upper Floridan aquifer water.

</div>

### How Hard Is Tampa Water?

The Tampa Water Department's 2025 water quality report put average total hardness at 12.3 grains per gallon, about 211 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard. The department's 2024 report put the average at 185 mg/L, or 10.8 grains per gallon, so the figure moves from year to year. The City of Tampa says hardness is lower in the wet season and higher in the dry season, between about 140 and 300 ppm, or 8 to 17 grains per gallon, so the number at your tap can differ from the annual average. A free test at your address gives the figure that matters for choosing equipment.

### What Hard Water Does to a Home

- **Appliances:** Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines. A heater coated in scale works harder to heat the same water.
- **Pipes and fixtures:** Mineral deposits narrow pipes, clog showerheads and leave white crust on faucets.
- **Skin and hair:** Hard water reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull.
- **Laundry:** Minerals stiffen fabric, dull colors and reduce how well detergent cleans.
- **Dishes and glass:** Spots and cloudy film appear after the dishwasher runs.

Hard water is a quality and comfort problem. The Tampa Water Department's reports list results for regulated contaminants separately, and hardness is not a health-based limit.

### Solutions for Hard Water

Three options reduce the effects of hard water:

1. **A water softener** removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and gives true soft water. [Water Softener Installation in Tampa, FL](/water-softener-installation/) is the most complete fix for the whole house.
2. **A conditioner or descaler** changes how minerals form scale without removing them. It reduces scale with less maintenance but does not make water feel soft.
3. **Point-of-use treatment**, such as a shower filter or an under-sink system, improves one tap and leaves the rest of the house untreated.

Most Tampa homeowners choose a [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) softener when hardness is the main complaint. A test first confirms hardness and checks for iron, which affects [sizing](/water-softener-guides/water-softener-size-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<summary>Is Tampa water safe to drink?</summary>

The Tampa Water Department's water quality reports list results against federal limits. Hardness is a quality issue, not a health limit, and the reports cover regulated contaminants separately.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does a water softener remove minerals I need?</summary>

A softener swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium or potassium. Food supplies most of your minerals, and drinking water is a minor source.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why does my glassware spot even with a rinse aid?</summary>

Dissolved minerals dry on glass as white spots. Softened water stops the spotting at the source.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can hard water damage my water heater?</summary>

Scale insulates the heating element or tank bottom, which makes the heater work harder and shortens its life. Flushing helps, and softened water prevents most of the buildup.

</details>

<summary>Do homes on well water have harder water?</summary>

Often yes, since private wells draw directly from the aquifer. A test shows the hardness and checks for iron and other minerals.
