## Why Hyde Park Residents Need a Water Softener

Much of Hyde Park dates from the National Register district's period of significance, which the National Park Service records as 1875 to 1949, with Late Victorian among the listed architectural styles. A home of that age can have original, repaired or fully replaced plumbing, so a free test and a look at your pipes decide what we recommend. Scale from very hard water narrows pipes, coats water heater elements and spots glass in homes of any age.

The Tampa Water Department's 2025 water quality report put average total hardness at 12.3 grains per gallon, about 211 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard, and its 2024 report put the average at 10.8 grains per gallon. At 12.3 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 3,900 grains daily, so a 32,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 8 days.

## About Hyde Park Water Quality

The Tampa Water Department draws primarily from the Hillsborough River, and it adds chlorine and then ammonia to create chloramine. Its 2025 report gave a daily average of 3.6 ppm for chlorine and chloramines and an average pH of 7.88. The City of Tampa says hardness runs between about 140 and 300 ppm, or 8 to 17 grains per gallon, lower in the wet season and higher in the dry season. The report is citywide and gives no Hyde Park figure, so a test at your tap gives the number for your home.
