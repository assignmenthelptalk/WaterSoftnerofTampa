## Which Contaminants Can a Filter Remove in Tampa, FL?

Six filter types target specific contaminants: lead and arsenic, chloramine and chlorine, fluoride, PFAS, nitrate and volatile organic compounds.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">



![Lead and Arsenic Removal Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-lead-and-arsenic-removal-tampa-fl.webp)



### Lead and Arsenic Removal

Lead enters drinking water mainly from old plumbing, and the EPA sets its health goal for lead at zero. Arsenic occurs naturally in some groundwater, and the EPA limit is 10 micrograms per liter. Under-sink [reverse osmosis systems](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) and certified filters reduce both. We recommend filters certified to the relevant NSF/ANSI standard and a test before and after installation.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Chloramine and Chlorine Removal Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-chloramine-and-chlorine-removal-tampa-fl.webp)



### Chloramine and Chlorine Removal

Chlorine and chloramine are disinfectants that can affect taste and smell. Standard carbon removes chlorine well but works slowly on chloramine, so catalytic carbon is used for chloramine. The Tampa Water Department adds chlorine and then ammonia to create chloramine, and its 2025 report gave a daily average of 3.6 ppm for chlorine and chloramines, within a range of 0.4 to 5.3 ppm and under the 4.0 ppm limit, so catalytic carbon is the safer choice for taste and smell.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Fluoride Filter Installation Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-fluoride-filter-installation-tampa-fl.webp)



### Fluoride Filter Installation

Fluoride occurs naturally in some groundwater, and the EPA enforceable limit is 4.0 milligrams per liter. Reverse osmosis and activated alumina media reduce fluoride. We install the type that suits your flow and budget.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![PFAS Water Filter Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-pfas-water-filter-tampa-fl.webp)



### PFAS Water Filter

PFAS are man-made chemicals. In 2024 the EPA set limits of 4 parts per trillion for PFOA and PFOS in public water systems. Activated carbon, ion exchange and reverse osmosis can reduce PFAS, and results depend on the product. [Verify current EPA limits before launch]

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Nitrate Filter Installation Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-nitrate-filter-installation-tampa-fl.webp)



### Nitrate Filter Installation

Nitrate comes from fertilizer, septic systems and livestock, and the EPA limit is 10 milligrams per liter as nitrogen. It matters most for infants and for [private wells](/well-water-softener-tampa-fl/) in farming areas. Ion exchange and reverse osmosis reduce nitrate, and boiling water does not.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter Tampa FL](../../assets/images/card-voc-volatile-organic-compound-filter-tampa-fl.webp)



### VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter

Volatile organic compounds are chemicals from fuels, solvents and industrial sources. Activated carbon is the standard treatment. A lab test confirms whether VOCs are present before you spend on a filter.

</div>

## When to Test for Contaminants

Test your water if you have a private well, an infant in the home, old plumbing, a change in taste or smell or a nearby source such as farming, fuel storage or industry. The Tampa Water Department's 2025 report describes water that leaves the system, and the Hillsborough River is its primary source. A test at your tap shows what reaches you. Ask which tests are free and which need an accredited lab, and what each costs.

> **Not a good fit if...** you have no test result. We do not sell a filter for a problem a test has not found.
