## Why Davis Islands Residents Need a Water Softener

Davis Islands was built from sand dredged out of the bay. Developer D. P. Davis began assembling the property in early 1924, a local history records that about 89 million cubic feet of sand was dredged, and the first 306 lots sold within three hours for $1,683,582. Many of the original homes are Mediterranean in style, and homes from the 1920s onward can have original, repaired or fully replaced plumbing, so a free test and a look at the pipes decide what we recommend.

The Tampa Water Department's 2025 water quality report put average total hardness at 12.3 grains per gallon, about 211 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard, and its 2024 report put the average at 10.8 grains per gallon. At 12.3 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 3,900 grains daily, so a 32,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 8 days.

## About Davis Islands Water Quality

The Tampa Water Department draws primarily from the Hillsborough River, and it adds chlorine and then ammonia to create chloramine. Its 2025 report gave a daily average of 3.6 ppm for chlorine and chloramines and an average pH of 7.88. The City of Tampa says hardness runs between about 140 and 300 ppm, or 8 to 17 grains per gallon, lower in the wet season and higher in the dry season. The report is citywide and gives no Davis Islands figure, so a test at your tap gives the number for your home.
