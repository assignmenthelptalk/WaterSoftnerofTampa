## Why Water Varies Across Tampa

Hardness changes through the year because the river is the primary source and its mineral content moves with the weather. The City of Tampa says hardness runs between about 140 and 300 ppm, or 8 to 17 grains per gallon, lower in the wet season and higher in the dry season, and the Tampa Water Department's 2025 report averaged 211 mg/L. The Tampa Water Department serves the City of Tampa and parts of unincorporated Hillsborough County, and homes outside its system, such as private wells, have their own water. A free water test at your tap gives the number that matters.
