## What Types of Drinking Water Systems Can You Install in Tampa, FL?

Three drinking water systems cover most homes: under sink systems, countertop purification units and alkaline water systems.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![Under Sink Drinking Water Systems Tampa FL](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Under Sink Drinking Water Systems

An under-sink drinking water system installs in the cabinet and delivers filtered water to a dedicated faucet. Options range from carbon filters to full [reverse osmosis](/reverse-osmosis-installation/). It gives the best balance of water quality, flow and convenience for a kitchen. The Tampa Water Department's primary source is the Hillsborough River, and many homeowners add an under-sink system to improve taste. A test at your tap gives the total dissolved solids number that decides whether a softener and an under-sink reverse osmosis system belong together.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Countertop Purification Units Tampa FL](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Countertop Purification Units

A countertop purification unit sits beside the sink and connects to the faucet or fills from a pitcher-style tank. It needs no plumbing work, so it suits renters and small spaces. The tradeoff is lower flow and a smaller capacity than an under-sink system.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Alkaline Water Systems Tampa FL](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Alkaline Water Systems

An alkaline water system adds a stage that raises the water's pH after [filtration](/water-filtration-systems/). It changes how the water tastes and measures on a pH test. Claims about health benefits are limited, so we explain what the stage does and does not do before you choose it. The Tampa Water Department's 2025 report put the average pH of finished water at 7.88, and a test at your tap gives your own reading.

</div>

</div>

### Bottleless Water Cooler Installation

A bottleless water cooler connects to your water line and filters water on demand, so you stop buying and lifting jugs. It suits offices, clinics and homes that use a lot of drinking water. We install the line, the drain if needed, and the filter set.

> **Not a good fit if...** you want to treat the whole house. A drinking water system treats one tap, so hardness elsewhere remains. A [Water Softener Systems in Tampa, FL](/water-softener-systems/) handles the rest.
