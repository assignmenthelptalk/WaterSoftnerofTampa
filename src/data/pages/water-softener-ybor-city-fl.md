## Why Ybor City Residents Need a Water Softener

Ybor City began in 1885, when Vicente Martinez-Ybor, a Spanish cigar manufacturer, started developing it, and the Florida historical marker records that 500 Cuban cigar makers sailed to Tampa on April 12, 1886. The district was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1974 and declared a National Historic Landmark District by the National Park Service in 1990. Buildings that old can have original, repaired or fully replaced plumbing, so a free test and a look at the pipes decide what we recommend.

The Tampa Water Department's 2025 water quality report put average total hardness at 12.3 grains per gallon, about 211 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as very hard, and its 2024 report put the average at 10.8 grains per gallon. At 12.3 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 3,900 grains daily, so a 32,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 8 days.

## About Ybor City Water Quality

The Tampa Water Department draws primarily from the Hillsborough River, and it adds chlorine and then ammonia to create chloramine. Its 2025 report gave a daily average of 3.6 ppm for chlorine and chloramines and an average pH of 7.88. The City of Tampa says hardness runs between about 140 and 300 ppm, or 8 to 17 grains per gallon, lower in the wet season and higher in the dry season. The report is citywide and gives no Ybor City figure, so a test at your tap gives the number for your building.
